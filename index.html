About FoodRescue

FoodRescue is a platform designed to reduce food wastage by connecting restaurants with NGOs and volunteers. Instead of letting surplus food go to waste, our platform helps redirect it to communities that need it. Together, we can make food redistribution easier and build a more sustainable future.
